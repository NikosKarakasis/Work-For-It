# 🎤 Presentation & Pitch Guide

> **Goal:** a clean 8-slide deck and a confident 3-minute pitch.
> Judges decide fast — your first 30 seconds matter more than your last feature.

---

## 🧱 The 8-Slide Structure

Copy this structure into Google Slides or Canva and fill it in.

| # | Slide | What goes on it | Time |
|---|-------|-----------------|------|
| 1 | Title | Project name, one-line tagline, team name | 10s |
| 2 | Problem | Who has the problem + why it matters (1 stat or story helps) | 30s |
| 3 | Solution | What you built, in plain words — no tech jargon yet | 30s |
| 4 | Demo | Switch to live demo or embedded video | 60s |
| 5 | How it works | Simple architecture/stack diagram | 20s |
| 6 | Impact | Who benefits, what changes for them | 15s |
| 7 | What's next | 2–3 future steps (shows the idea has legs) | 10s |
| 8 | Team + Thanks | Names, roles, contact / repo QR code | 5s |

**Total: ~3 minutes.** Practice once with a timer — everyone runs long the first time.

---

## 🗣 What to SAY (speaking guide)

**Slide 2 — Problem.** Open with the user, not the tech:
> "Every student knows this: it's 1 AM, you have 80 pages of notes, and no time to make study materials."

**Slide 3 — Solution.** One sentence formula:
> "[Product] helps [who] do [what] by [how]."

**Slide 4 — Demo.** Narrate what you click: "I upload my notes… and in 5 seconds we get flashcards." Silence during a demo kills energy.

**Slide 6 — Impact.** Numbers beat adjectives: "turns 2 hours of prep into 5 minutes."

---

## 🎨 Design Rules (the boring stuff that wins points)

- **Max ~20 words per slide.** Slides support you; they are not the script.
- One idea per slide. If a slide needs two ideas, make two slides.
- Big font (24pt+), high contrast, consistent colors.
- Screenshots > text. Show the product everywhere you can.
- Spell-check the title slide. (Seriously.)

---

## 🧯 Demo Insurance

Live demos break. Protect yourself:

1. Record a backup screen capture of the happy path BEFORE presenting.
2. Have the app already open and logged in — never start from a cold login screen.
3. If Wi-Fi dies, narrate over the backup video like nothing happened.

---

## ❓ Q&A Prep

Judges usually ask one of these — decide your answers as a team:

- "What was the hardest technical part?"
- "How is this different from [existing product]?"
- "Who would pay for this / who's the user?"
- "What would you build next?"
